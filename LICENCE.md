## ✨ Features

### ESP
- Tile Info Panel — inspect terrain, yields, and improvements
- Map Layer Switcher — toggle resource and border overlays
- Visibility Notes — review explored areas in single-player study
- Camera Speed — adjust map pan and zoom for study (0.1x to 5x)

### Wallhack
- Resource Catalog — browse luxuries, strategics, and bonuses
- Unit Tracker Notes — organize army and civilian units
- Leader Panel Notes — review leader abilities and agendas

### Aimbot
- District Planner Notes — segment study on any city layout
- Production Queue Notes — plan build order in the editor
- Yield Buffer Notes — chain adjacency bonuses in the planner
